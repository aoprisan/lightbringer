Move with the joystick or WASD to walk and weave around the menhirs. Stand STILL to trace the Elder Sign — a sigil that banishes the host around you; move to dodge and it fades. But tracing frays your MIND, and the host's nearness bleeds it — seal the ward-stones and gather the clue-motes to steady your sanity. Banish every horror to seal the threshold; lose your health and you are slain, lose your sanity and you go mad.
