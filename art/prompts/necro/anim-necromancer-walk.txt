--- WHAT THIS IS ---
The necromancer is the player avatar. Today it draws as ONE static sprite
(art/necromancer.png) and slides across the ground when moving. These two frames
are the stride extremes of a 3-frame walk cycle: the game plays
  stepA  →  necromancer.png (neutral)  →  stepB  →  necromancer.png  →  …
while the necromancer is moving, and the neutral base sprite alone when standing
still. So you are NOT inventing a new character — you are nudging the EXISTING
sprite into two mid-stride poses.

--- GENERATION RULES (read once) ---
- Use Gemini's IMAGE-EDIT mode on art/necromancer.png for BOTH frames. Do NOT
  generate from scratch — continuity beats per-frame beauty.
- Keep EVERYTHING fixed except the gait: same hood, same robes, same staff and
  soul-lantern, same colours, same lighting, same SIZE/SCALE, and the same
  CENTER point (the body's mass stays centered in the 1024 square). Only the
  legs/robe-hem and a small body bob change. Misregistered frames will jitter
  when the game swaps them.
- No transparency. Flat solid #060B08 (graveyard green-black) background, exactly
  like the base sprite. Never white — the green glow would halo.
- Face SCREEN-RIGHT (or stay front-facing 3/4 as the base does). The game mirrors
  the sprite horizontally to face left, so do not bake in a left-facing pose.
- Three-quarter top-down (~60°), the same camera as the base sprite.
- Readable at small sizes: the stride must read as a SILHOUETTE change (robe-hem
  swing, lean, staff bob), not fine detail.
