Steer with the joystick or WASD — your bomber can never stop. HOLD A STRAIGHT AND LEVEL COURSE to arm the bombsight; an armed sight releases bombs ahead of the nose. But the flak leads a straight run perfectly — break away from the red reticles before they burst. Fighters scramble from their airfields (bomb a field first and its squadron burns on the ground); your escorts fly cover and tangle with the rest. Targets silenced in quick succession run the crew hot — an ACE STREAK that sharpens your bombs and opens the engines. And heed the defence KLAXON: on its cry the nearest grounded squadron is vectored straight onto you, faster and more often as the target list thins. Silence every target to complete the raid; lose your airframe and you go down.
