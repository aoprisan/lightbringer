=== 9f. Per-city sprite set — THE PALE BASTION (fortified, warded) ===

Re-skins the city's BUILT WORLD so The Pale Bastion reads as a fortified ward-
quarter: pale chalk-white stone, blank curtain walls, cold blue ward-sigils. Same
silhouettes as the base sprites — only the material changes — so each still reads
as its kind. This city's SIGNATURE structure is the ward-obelisk, so its re-skin
is included here; it does not use lightwells.

OUTPUT FOLDER: art/bastion/    (the loader tries this city folder, then falls back
to the base art/<name>.png). Files to produce, same names as the base set:
  ground.png  dwelling-dark.png  dwelling-lit.png  dwelling-awakened.png
  dwelling-snuffed.png  conduit.png  press.png  shrine.png  obelisk.png

SHARED, DO NOT re-skin (universal forces): keeper-node, keeper-patrol,
player-lantern, veil-scar, flame-spark, obelisk-spent (the cracked face is
universal), font. The Bastion reuses the base ones.

RULES (read once):
- No transparency. Generate every sprite isolated on a flat solid #0B0E1A.
  Never a white background.
- 1024×1024, square, single centered subject, no text, no watermark, no border.
- DWELLING CONTINUITY: generate dwelling-dark FIRST, then image-edit it for
  lit / awakened / snuffed, so all four share one silhouette.
- ground.png must tile SEAMLESSLY, edge to edge, no focal point.

Each block below is self-contained — copy any one whole.

--- bastion/ground.png ---
Style: hand-painted dark-fantasy game asset, night, ink-and-gilt feel. Palette: deep indigo #0B0E1A, pale chalk-white and grey stone; COLD steel-blue ward-light #9fc4e8; warm candle-gold #F5C66B only for the rare lit thing. Soft edges, no outlines. View: top-down. Single subject, flat solid #0B0E1A background, 1024×1024, no text, no border.
Seamless tileable texture of a fortress courtyard floor seen from straight above at night: pale dressed chalk-white flagstones laid in a strict military grid, faint cold-blue sigil-lines inlaid here and there, swept severe stone. Very low contrast, cold and devout, no focal point, no warm light, edges must tile seamlessly. 1024×1024.

--- bastion/dwelling-dark.png (GENERATE THIS FIRST) ---
Style as above, light from its own flame only. View: three-quarter top-down ~60°. Single centered subject, flat solid #0B0E1A background, 1024×1024, no text, no border.
A small fortified cell-house of pale chalk-white stone, steep slate roof, one tall narrow arrow-slit window, a heavy banded door, an empty iron lamp-bracket. Completely unlit and cold. Pale blue-grey, almost a silhouette, a line of cold moonlight on the ridge.

--- bastion/dwelling-lit.png (IMAGE-EDIT of dwelling-dark) ---
Edit this image: the same pale cell, now lit from within. Warm candle-gold glowing through the narrow arrow-slit and under the door, a small warm pool on the pale flags — a forbidden warmth in a cold fortress. The stone stays pale and cold; only the light is warm. Keep the exact same silhouette and position. Flat solid #0B0E1A background, no text, 1024×1024.

--- bastion/dwelling-awakened.png (IMAGE-EDIT of dwelling-dark) ---

Serene and defiant — a soul awake inside the ward. Same silhouette and position. Flat solid #0B0E1A background, no text, 1024×1024.
