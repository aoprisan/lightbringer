Pentagram sigil — the spinoff's signature weapon (PROCEDURAL, no PNG shipped).

The pentagram in the action-combat spinoff (pentagram.html / pentagram.ts) is
drawn procedurally as layered SVG, NOT from a PNG — so there is no art file to
generate. This prompt records the intended look in case it is ever painted as a
sprite later (it would drop in via the same spriteFor() fallback the rest of the
art uses).

Intended look:
- A {5/2} star polygon (the classic single-stroke pentagram) inscribed in a
  circle, centered under the flame-hero.
- Inscribes itself as the hero stands still: scales up and brightens with
  "charge" (0 -> 1), fades when the hero moves.
- Colour: an infernal warm palette to set it apart from the parent game's gold
  light — ember red (#ff6a3c) outer ring, gold (#ffd87a) star stroke, a soft
  red-gold radial fill (#ffd9a0 -> #ff6a3c -> transparent), all through a glow.
- Slowly rotating. Reads as a burning brand scorched into the ground.

If ever shipped as a PNG: transparent background, square, drawn on solid night
(#0B0E1A) like the other sprites so the spriteFade mask melts its edge, and add
it to sw.js ASSETS + bump CACHE.
