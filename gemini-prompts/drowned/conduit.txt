Style: hand-painted dark-fantasy game asset, medieval city at night, ink-and-gilt feel. Palette: deep indigo #0B0E1A, slate blue, charcoal; warm gold #F5C66B for any light. Soft edges, no outlines. View: three-quarter top-down ~60°. Single centered subject, flat solid #0B0E1A background, 1024×1024, no text, no watermark, no border.
A flooded medieval canal-alley seen from above: an open black water channel running between sodden algae-stained walls, a floating sheen of lamp-oil and a few drifting scraps of paper on the surface, mooring ropes and message-strings sagging overhead. Dark and wet, the faintest cold reflection running the length of the channel hinting it would carry light across the water.
