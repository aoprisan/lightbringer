=== 13a. Building sprite — the lightwell (font) ===
A new built-world object for The Burning Vigil: a LIGHTWELL, a stone well-mouth
brimming with molten gold flame drawn up from the earth. In play it is PASSABLE
(the hero stands in it) and its aura lets the sigil inscribe even while moving.
Universal base sprite (cities may re-skin it in their own folder). The render
falls back to a plain shape when this PNG is absent, so it is optional.
Output: art/font.png

RULES:
- No transparency. Isolated on a flat solid #0B0E1A background. Never white.
- 1024×1024, square, single centered subject, no text, no watermark, no border.
- View: three-quarter top-down ~60°, lit by its own welling flame.

Style: hand-painted dark-fantasy game asset, medieval city at night, ink-and-gilt illuminated-manuscript feel. Palette: deep indigo #0B0E1A, slate blue, charcoal stone; warm gold #e8b34b and #ffd87a with a near-white hot core #fff2cf. Soft painterly edges, no outlines, no photorealism.
A circular carved stone well-mouth set into the ground, brimming to the rim with molten, luminous gold flame that wells gently upward and spills a warm glow and faint runnels of light onto the stones around it. Low and broad, inviting to stand beside, the brightest warm thing in the gloom — a wellspring of standing fire, not a bonfire. Single centered subject, flat solid #0B0E1A background, 1024×1024.
