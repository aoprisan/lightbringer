The Necromancer's March

The Light-Bringer The Burning Vigil The Watcher at the Threshold The Moon's Hunger
Move with the joystick or WASD to march and weave around the wells and altars. March onto an open grave to raise skeletons (they cost souls); they hunt the nearest knight on their own. Raze the houses to heal the horde, and gather the soul-wisps the fallen leave. Overrun every knight to take the village.
