# art/pathway.png  (the Burning Vigil walkway / speed-lane tile)

The Burning Vigil (pentagram.ts) lays "pathways" — open swept lanes the
flame-hero runs swift along to kite the swarm. The render TILES this sprite down
the length of each lane (an SVG pattern, ~60px tile), so it must be SEAMLESSLY
TILEABLE left-to-right: the LEFT and RIGHT edges have to wrap perfectly with no
visible seam. One row tall is all that shows (the tile's full height is the lane
width), so the top and bottom edges fade naturally into the dark ground.

RULES:
- Base palette (warm candle-gold, indigo night) — NOT a city re-skin. This is a
  universal terrain texture, shared across every city like the Keepers.
- SEAMLESS HORIZONTAL TILE: left edge must continue into the right edge exactly
  (a repeating cobbled run). Do not center a single feature — design it to repeat.
- The lane reads as a CLEARED, WORN STREET: a swept stone/cobble road, paler and
  smoother than the surrounding rubble, faint cart-rut grooves running left-to-
  right along its length, a hint of warm reflected lamplight on the stones. A
  road you run FAST down — open, inviting, unobstructed. No people, no props.
- 1024×1024, square, no text, no watermark, no border.
- Top-down ~60° three-quarter view, matching the base sprite set.
- The road occupies the central horizontal band; let the very top and bottom
  edges sink toward the indigo night #0B0E1A so the lane blends into the ground.

PROMPT:
A seamless horizontally-tiling top-down texture of a cleared medieval cobbled
street — a swept worn stone road running left to right across the frame, pale
warm grey-gold flagstones smoother and brighter than the dark rubble around them,
shallow cart-rut grooves tracking along its length, faint warm candle-gold #F5C66B
lamplight glinting off the wet stones. An open, inviting, unobstructed lane meant
to be run down fast. The left and right edges must wrap seamlessly so the road
repeats endlessly when tiled. The top and bottom edges fade down into deep indigo
night #0B0E1A. No people, no carts, no props, no text. 1024×1024.
