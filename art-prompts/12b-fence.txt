# art/fence.png  (the Burning Vigil obstacle / fence-barricade tile)

The Burning Vigil (pentagram.ts) strings "fences" — low linear walls the hero and
shades must weave around for cover (they block bodies, NOT the pentagram's flame,
which burns straight through). The render TILES this sprite down the length of
each fence segment (an SVG pattern, ~26px tile), so it must be SEAMLESSLY
TILEABLE left-to-right: the LEFT and RIGHT edges have to wrap perfectly with no
visible seam. One row tall is all that shows (the tile's full height is the wall's
drawn thickness), so the top and bottom edges fade into the dark ground.

RULES:
- Base palette (warm candle-gold, indigo night) — NOT a city re-skin. This is a
  universal terrain texture, shared across every city like the Keepers.
- SEAMLESS HORIZONTAL TILE: left edge must continue into the right edge exactly
  (a repeating run of barricade). Do not center a single feature — design it to
  repeat into a continuous wall.
- It reads as a LOW SOLID BLOCKER: a stout barricade of dark weathered timber
  posts and planks (or piled rubble / a broken stone wall), chunky and opaque, a
  faint cool indigo top-edge highlight so it reads as raised cover, a touch of
  warm reflected firelight catching its near face. Clearly something you take
  cover BEHIND — heavy, impassable, grim. No gaps, no people.
- 1024×1024, square, no text, no watermark, no border.
- Top-down ~60° three-quarter view, matching the base sprite set.
- The barricade fills the central horizontal band; the very top and bottom edges
  sink toward the indigo night #0B0E1A so the wall blends into the ground.
