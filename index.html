The Light-Bringer

One world, taught that light burns. Five natures walk it — four on the ground and one in the leaden sky — and you must choose which to wear before you descend.

Choose your class

The Burning Vigil Stand still to inscribe a burning pentagram and scorch the city's risen watch. Clear the host to cleanse the city. The Necromancer's March Raise a horde from the village graves and command the dead. Raze the houses, overrun the knights, claim the village. The Watcher at the Threshold Trace the Elder Sign to banish the eldritch host — but guard your sanity as well as your life. Seal the threshold. The Moon's Hunger Stalk a fog-bound village unseen; by moonlight turn beast and run the watch down. Cut down the host to claim the village. The Iron Rain Captain a heavy bomber that cannot stop: hold the run to arm the sight, dodge the flak, and let your escorts hold off the fighters.
The Sin-Eater's Roundprototype A rite under trial: your footsteps lay a guttering line of salt — cross your own line to close the circle, and everything unshriven inside is judged.
